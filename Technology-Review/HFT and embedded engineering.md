### **⚙️ Why HFT Firms Like Embedded Engineers**

- You **think in terms of cycles**, not milliseconds.

- You know **how hardware behaves under load** — cache misses, branch mispredictions, NUMA effects.

- You can **optimize I/O paths** — just like optimizing SPI/I²C buses in devices, but now it’s network sockets and syscalls.

- You’re **comfortable with low-level tuning**: memory layout, thread affinity, lock-free structures.
